sCollectPlay Lite

Free: choose a folder and play – no import, no copying

Free · In the Microsoft Store from November 2026

Free: choose a folder and play – music, audiobooks, podcasts, films and e-books fall into order by themselves. No import, no account, no cloud. iTunes libraries (XML), too.

sCollectPlay Lite plays your collection where it lives: in any folder, on external drives or on a network drive. Choose a folder, and music, audiobooks, podcasts, films and e-books appear sorted in one list – no import, no copying, and nothing about your files changes.

24 languages: English, German, Czech, Croatian, Dutch, Finnish, French, Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian Bokmål, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). The app follows the Windows display language.

This edition plays one source per session: a folder or an XML. Opening another one replaces the previous one.

TWO SOURCES

  • Folders: sCollectPlay Lite searches a folder and its subfolders and reads title, artist, album, genre and duration from the files themselves. “Refresh Scanned Folders” (Ctrl+R) then reads only what is new, changed or removed.
  • iTunes XML: the library of iTunes for Windows with playlists, ratings and play counts. Whether a track is music, an audiobook, a podcast, a feature film, a series or a music video, the app takes from the XML.

ORDER WITHOUT EFFORT

A folder doesn’t say whether a file is a song or an audiobook. That is why sCollectPlay Lite sorts by length when scanning: audio up to 10, 10–30, 30–60 and over 60 minutes, video from short clips to films over three and a half hours. Songs, radio-play episodes and audiobooks end up apart without you having to set anything. E-books and PDFs the app recognizes by their extension.

FINDING

  • Search across title, artist, album, genre and more – inside playlists, too

PLAYBACK

  • Audio with queue, shuffle and repeat
  • Videos in their own window, with slow motion, frame-by-frame stepping, audio track and subtitle selection and full screen
  • Windows plays most formats itself – MP3, AAC, FLAC, OGG, Opus and WMA as well as MP4, MKV, AVI, WMV and MPG. What it doesn’t know, such as AIFF, DSD or FLV, the app hands to the program set in Windows for that file type – VLC, for example

MORE IN THE FULL VERSION

sCollectPlay combines several folders into one collection, remembers the last source across restarts and offers a column browser by genre, artist and album, freely chosen columns, your own and smart playlists, and export of playlists as a folder with an M3U8 list for VLC and devices.

YOUR FILES STAY AS THEY ARE

sCollectPlay Lite writes no tags and changes no metadata. It deletes only on your explicit command: a media file from scanned folders or an iTunes XML then goes to the Recycle Bin (on drives without one, such as network drives, it is deleted permanently after confirmation). Files in a sCollect library are never deleted by the app.

ON YOUR PC, AND NOWHERE ELSE

No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising. The app accesses only the folders you give it. Every language with complete help.
